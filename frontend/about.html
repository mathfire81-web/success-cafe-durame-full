How It Started

From a small bakery counter to one of Durame’s favorite kitchens.

Success Cafe began as a modest bakery counter with two tables, one oven, and a simple promise: bake everything fresh, every single day. Word spread quickly around Durame town, and the small counter grew into a full kitchen serving pastries at sunrise, wood-fired pizza and hand-pressed burgers through the day, and machiatos that keep the regulars coming back long after closing time.

Today, Success Cafe is still run the same way it started - with real ingredients, honest recipes, and a team that treats every order like it's for family. Whether you're dining in, taking away, or ordering delivery, you're getting the same care that opened our doors in the first place.

    How to Order at Success Cafe

    Whether you're craving delivery or stopping by in person, here's exactly how it works.

    Ordering for Delivery

    Hot food, straight to your door

    1. Add to your cart

      Browse the Menu or use the Delivery panel's food picker to add what you're craving.

    2. Choose delivery & address

      Open your cart, select "Delivery," and drop your address in Durame town.

    3. Verify your payment

      Pay with Telebirr, CBE Birr, bank transfer, or cash, then confirm your order.

    4. We bring it to you

      Sit back - hot food arrives at your door in about 30 minutes.

    Ordering In the Cafe

    Walk in, sit down, dig in

    1. Walk in or browse ahead

      Visit us in Durame town, or check the Menu online before you arrive.

    2. Tell us what you'd like

      Order at the counter, or add items to your cart and choose "Pickup."

    3. Pay your way

      Cash, Telebirr, CBE Birr, or bank transfer - whatever's easiest for you.

    4. Enjoy, fresh & fast

      We'll seat you or call your name - ready in about 20 minutes.
