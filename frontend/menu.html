    Ordering for Delivery (ወደ ቤቶ ለማዘዝ)

    Hot food, straight to your door

    1. Add to your cart

      Browse the Menu or use the Delivery panel's food picker to add what you're craving.(በዴሊቨሪ ፔጅ ምግቦትን ይምረጡ)

    2. Choose delivery & address

      Open your cart, select "Delivery," and drop your address in Durame town. (ካርት ይጫኑ እና ያረጋግጡ)

    3. Verify your payment

      Pay with Telebirr, CBE Birr, bank transfer, or cash, then confirm your order. (የከፈሉበትን ደረሰኝ ያስገቡ እና ያረጋግጡ)

    4. We bring it to you

      Sit back - hot food arrives at your door in about 40 minutes.(እንዳሉበት ቦታ ርቀት ከ 40-70 ደቂቃ ዉስጥ እስካሉበት እናደርሳለን )

    Ordering In the Cafe (ካፌ መጥተዉ ለማዘዝ)

    Walk in, sit down, dig in

    1. Walk in or browse ahead

      Visit us in Durame town, or check the Menu online before you arrive. (ሜኑ ተጭነው ምግቦትን ይምረጡ)

    2. Tell us what you'd like

      Order at the counter, or add items to your cart and choose "Pickup." (የመረጡትን ያረጋግጡ)

    3. Pay your way

      Cash, Telebirr, CBE Birr, or bank transfer - whatever's easiest for you. (የከፈሉበትን ደረሰኝ ያስገቡ እና ያረጋግጡ)

    4. Enjoy, fresh & fast

      We'll seat you or call your name - ready in about 20 minutes. (በ20 ደቂቃ እናደርሳለን)
